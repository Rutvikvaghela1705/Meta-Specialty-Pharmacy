Skin Care Services

Advanced Treatments for Healthy, Radiant Skin

Acne & Eczema Treatment

Acne and eczema treatments focus on calming inflammation, soothing the skin, and preventing flare-ups.

Skin Allergy Testing

Skin Allergy Testing involves applying small allergens to the skin and observing reactions, usually taking about 15–30 minutes.

Anti-aging Treatments

Reduce wrinkles, fine lines, and other signs of aging using creams, serums, laser therapy, or minimally invasive procedures to rejuvenate and tighten the skin.

Laser Therapy

Laser therapy uses focused light to treat skin issues like wrinkles, scars, pigmentation, and hair removal by targeting specific skin layers for rejuvenation and repair.

Skin Cancer Screening

Skin cancer screening is a quick, painless exam where a doctor checks your skin for unusual moles or spots to detect early signs of cancer.

Psoriasis & fungal infection

Psoriasis is a chronic skin condition causing red, scaly patches due to immune system activity, while fungal infections are caused by fungi leading to itchy, irritated skin.
